# PATIENT ADVOCATE DESIGNATION

## OF AHMED R. HASSAN

State of Michigan · MCL 700.5506 et seq. · Michigan Mental Health Code, MCL 330.1404 Federal HIPAA, 45 C.F.R. Part 164 · Michigan Uniform Anatomical Gift Act, MCL 333.10101 et seq.

I, AHMED R. HASSAN, a resident of Dearborn, Michigan, being of sound mind, designate the individual named below as my patient advocate under MCL 700.5506, to make care and treatment decisions for me if I become unable to participate in those decisions myself.

## SECTION 1 - DESIGNATION OF PATIENT ADVOCATE

I designate RAGA HASSAN, my wife, as my patient advocate. If RAGA HASSAN is unable or unwilling to serve, I designate KARIM HASSAN, my brother, as my successor patient advocate.

## SECTION 2 - WHEN THIS DESIGNATION TAKES EFFECT

My patient advocate may act only after my attending physician and one other physician have examined me and determined, in writing, that I am unable to participate in medical treatment decisions.

## SECTION 3 - POWERS GRANTED

My patient advocate is authorized to make care and treatment decisions on my behalf, to consent to or refuse any care, treatment, service, or procedure, to authorize my admission to or discharge from any hospital, nursing facility, or other care facility, and to arrange for services in my home.

## SECTION 4 - ACCESS TO MEDICAL RECORDS AND HIPAA AUTHORIZATION

I authorize my patient advocate to request, receive, review, and copy any information regarding my physical or mental health, including all protected health information governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. This authorization applies to every physician, hospital, care facility, insurer, and pharmacy holding such information.

## SECTION 5 - LIFE-SUSTAINING TREATMENT

I have discussed my wishes regarding mechanical ventilation, artificial nutrition and hydration, cardiopulmonary resuscitation, and other life-sustaining measures with my patient advocate.

I acknowledge that this designation allows my patient advocate to make decisions that could allow me to pass, and I expressly authorize my patient advocate to exercise that authority in accordance with my wishes as stated in this document.

## SECTION 6 - ORGAN AND TISSUE DONATION

Upon my passing, I wish to donate any needed organs and tissues for the purpose of transplantation, therapy, medical research, or education, pursuant to the Michigan Uniform Anatomical Gift Act.

## SECTION 7 - REVOCATION

I may revoke this designation at any time and in any manner by which I am able to communicate my intent to revoke it.

## SECTION 8 - GOVERNING LAW

This designation shall be governed by the laws of the State of Michigan.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have executed this Patient Advocate Designation on this [DATE LINE].
