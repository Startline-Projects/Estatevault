# THE AHMED R. HASSAN AND RAGA HASSAN REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST

This Trust Agreement is made on the date set forth below between AHMED R. HASSAN and RAGA HASSAN, of Dearborn, Michigan, as Grantors, and AHMED R. HASSAN and RAGA HASSAN, as initial Trustees.

## ARTICLE I - CREATION OF TRUST

1.1 Name of Trust

This trust shall be known as THE AHMED R. HASSAN AND RAGA HASSAN REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST.

1.2 Transfer of Property

The Grantors transfer to the Trustees the property described in Schedule A attached to this Agreement, to be held, administered, and distributed according to the terms set forth below.

## ARTICLE II - REVOCATION AND AMENDMENT

2.1 Right to Revoke

The Grantors reserve the right to revoke this trust in whole or in part at any time during their joint lifetimes, exercisable jointly and not individually, by written instrument delivered to the Trustees.

2.2 Right to Amend

The Grantors reserve the right to amend any provision of this trust at any time during their joint lifetimes by written instrument delivered to the Trustees.

## ARTICLE III - TRUSTEES

3.1 Initial Trustees

The Grantors, AHMED R. HASSAN and RAGA HASSAN, shall serve as the initial Trustees of this trust.

3.2 First Successor Trustee

Upon the resignation, incapacity, or passing of either initial Trustee, the surviving initial Trustee shall continue to serve alone. Upon the resignation, incapacity, or passing of both initial Trustees, KARIM HASSAN shall serve as First Successor Trustee.

3.3 Backup Successor Trustee

If KARIM HASSAN is unable or unwilling to serve, NADIA HASSAN shall serve as Backup Successor Trustee.

3.4 No Bond Required

No Trustee serving under this Agreement shall be required to furnish bond or other security in any jurisdiction.

## ARTICLE IV - ADMINISTRATION DURING THE GRANTORS' LIVES

4.1 Distributions to the Grantors

During the joint lives of the Grantors, the Trustees shall distribute to the Grantors, or apply for their benefit, such amounts of net income and principal as the Grantors may direct in writing from time to time.

4.2 Incapacity of a Grantor

If a Grantor becomes unable to manage their own affairs, as certified in writing by two licensed physicians, the Trustees shall distribute or apply so much of the net income and principal as the Trustees determine to be appropriate for the health, support, maintenance, and comfort of that Grantor.

## ARTICLE V - ADMINISTRATION AFTER THE PASSING OF A GRANTOR

5.1 Survivor's Interest

Upon the passing of the first Grantor, the trust estate shall continue to be held for the benefit of the surviving Grantor, who shall retain the right to revoke or amend the trust with respect to their own contributed property.

5.2 Payment of Expenses

The Trustees may pay from the trust estate the expenses of last illness, funeral and burial expenses, valid debts, and taxes attributable to the trust estate.

## ARTICLE VI - SPECIFIC GIFTS

6.1 Tangible Personal Property

Upon the passing of the surviving Grantor, the Trustees shall distribute the grandfather's pocket watch to OMAR HASSAN and the wedding jewelry to LAYLA HASSAN, if each survives by thirty (30) days.

6.2 Lapse

If a beneficiary named in this Article does not survive by thirty (30) days, the gift shall lapse and become part of the residuary trust estate.

## ARTICLE VII - DISTRIBUTION OF TRUST ESTATE

7.1 Primary Distribution

Upon the passing of the surviving Grantor, the Trustees shall distribute the remaining trust estate to LAYLA HASSAN and OMAR HASSAN, in equal shares, being fifty percent (50%) to each.

7.2 Contingent Distribution

If a named beneficiary does not survive by thirty (30) days, that beneficiary's share shall be distributed to their descendants, per stirpes. If there are no such descendants, the share shall be distributed to the surviving named beneficiaries in equal shares.

7.3 Distributions to Minors

If any beneficiary entitled to a distribution has not attained twenty-five (25) years of age, the Trustees shall retain that beneficiary's share in a separate trust and distribute income and principal for the beneficiary's health, education, maintenance, and support until the beneficiary attains twenty-five (25) years of age.

## ARTICLE VIII - TRUSTEE POWERS

8.1 General Powers

The Trustees shall have all powers granted to trustees under the Michigan Trust Code, including the power to retain, invest, sell, exchange, lease, mortgage, and otherwise deal with trust property, to borrow money, to employ professionals, to allocate receipts and disbursements between income and principal, and to make distributions in cash or in kind.

8.2 Digital Assets

The Trustees are authorized to access, manage, distribute, copy, delete, or terminate any digital asset or digital account held in the name of the trust, pursuant to the Michigan Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act.

## ARTICLE IX - SPENDTHRIFT PROVISION

No beneficiary shall have the power to anticipate, encumber, or transfer any interest in the trust estate in advance of actual distribution, and no interest shall be subject to the claims of any creditor of a beneficiary.

## ARTICLE X - GOVERNING LAW

This Trust Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Michigan.

## ARTICLE XI - SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Trust Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
