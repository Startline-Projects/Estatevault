# LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT

## OF AHMED R. HASSAN

I, AHMED R. HASSAN, a resident of Dearborn, Michigan, being of sound mind and memory, do hereby declare this to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils.

## ARTICLE I - FAMILY IDENTIFICATION

I am married to Raga Hassan. I have two children now living, namely Layla Hassan and Omar Hassan.

## ARTICLE II - IDENTIFICATION OF TRUST

I refer in this Will to THE AHMED R. HASSAN AND RAGA HASSAN REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST, established on the same date as this Will, of which I am a Grantor and an initial Trustee, together with any amendments made to it.

## ARTICLE III - POUR-OVER RESIDUARY CLAUSE

3.1 Pour-Over Gift

I give, devise, and bequeath all of my estate, both real and personal, of whatever kind and wherever situated, that I may own or be entitled to at the time of my passing, to the then-acting Trustee of THE AHMED R. HASSAN AND RAGA HASSAN REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST, to be added to, administered, and distributed as part of said Trust in accordance with its terms as they exist at the time of my passing, or as thereafter amended.

3.2 Trust Terms Control

The property poured over under this Article shall be administered and distributed according to the terms of the Trust and not according to the terms of this Will.

3.3 Backup Distribution

If for any reason the Trust is not in existence at the time of my passing, or if the pour-over gift made in Section 3.1 fails, then I give, devise, and bequeath my residuary estate to my trust beneficiaries in the shares set forth in the Trust.

## ARTICLE IV - APPOINTMENT OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

I nominate and appoint my wife, Raga Hassan, as the Personal Representative of this Will. If she is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate and appoint my brother, Karim Hassan, as successor Personal Representative. I request that no bond be required.

My Personal Representative shall have full authority under MCL 700.3715 to manage my estate during administration and to transfer assets to the Trustee of the Trust.

## ARTICLE V - GUARDIAN DESIGNATION

If my spouse does not survive me, I nominate Karim Hassan as guardian of the person and estate of my minor children, and Nadia Hassan as successor guardian.

## ARTICLE VI - GOVERNING LAW

This Will shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Michigan.

## ARTICLE VII - SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Will is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

## ARTICLE VIII - EXECUTION INSTRUCTIONS

EXECUTION INSTRUCTIONS: Michigan law (MCL 700.2502) requires the testator to sign in the presence of two (2) adult witnesses who are present at the same time. Witnesses must sign in the testator's presence. Witnesses should not be named beneficiaries. All three parties should sign simultaneously in the same location.
