# LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT

## OF AHMED R. HASSAN

I, AHMED R. HASSAN, a resident of Dearborn, Michigan, being of sound mind and memory, do hereby declare this to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils.

### ARTICLE I - FAMILY IDENTIFICATION

I am married to Raga Hassan. I have two children now living, namely Layla Hassan and Omar Hassan. All references in this Will to "my children" include any child later born to or adopted by me.

## ARTICLE II - PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Personal Representative to pay all of my legally enforceable debts, the expenses of my last illness, the expenses of administration of my estate, and all estate, inheritance, and succession taxes payable by reason of my passing, out of the residue of my estate, without apportionment.

## ARTICLE III - SPECIFIC GIFTS

I give and bequeath my grandfather's pocket watch (item #3 on my personal property schedule) to my son, Omar Hassan, if he survives me by thirty (30) days. I give and bequeath my wife's wedding jewelry to my daughter, Layla Hassan, if she survives me by thirty (30) days.

If any beneficiary named in this Article does not survive me by thirty (30) days, the gift to that beneficiary shall lapse and become part of my residuary estate.

## ARTICLE IV - RESIDUARY ESTATE

I give, devise, and bequeath all of the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate, both real and personal, of whatever kind and wherever situated, to my wife, **Raga Hassan**, if she survives me by thirty (30) days.

If Raga Hassan does not survive me by thirty (30) days, the entire residuary estate shall pass to my children, Layla Hassan and Omar Hassan, in equal shares.

## ARTICLE V - CONTINGENT BENEFICIARIES

If neither my wife nor any of my children survive me by thirty (30) days, then I give my entire residuary estate to my brother, Karim Hassan, and my sister, Nadia Hassan, in equal shares. If any contingent beneficiary shall predecease me, their share shall pass to the surviving contingent beneficiaries in equal shares.

## ARTICLE VI - APPOINTMENT OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

I nominate and appoint my wife, Raga Hassan, as the Personal Representative of this Will. If she is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate and appoint my brother, Karim Hassan, as successor Personal Representative. I request that no bond be required of any Personal Representative named herein.

## ARTICLE VII - POWERS OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

My Personal Representative shall have full authority under the Michigan Estates and Protected Individuals Code, MCL 700.3715, including but not limited to the power to sell, lease, mortgage, or otherwise deal with estate property, to pay debts and expenses, to distribute assets in kind or in cash, to employ attorneys, accountants, and other professionals, and to compromise, settle, or abandon claims in favor of or against my estate, all without the necessity of court supervision where permitted by Michigan law.

## ARTICLE VIII - GUARDIAN DESIGNATION

If my spouse does not survive me, I nominate Karim Hassan as guardian of the person and estate of my minor children. If Karim Hassan is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate Nadia Hassan as successor guardian. I request that no bond be required of any guardian named herein.

## ARTICLE IX - NO-CONTEST CLAUSE

Any beneficiary who contests this Will, or who assists another in contesting this Will, shall forfeit their share, and that share shall be distributed among the remaining beneficiaries proportionally as though the contesting beneficiary had not survived me.

## ARTICLE X - DIGITAL ASSETS

I authorize my Personal Representative to access, manage, distribute, copy, delete, or terminate any digital asset, digital account, or electronically stored information owned by me, including but not limited to email accounts, social media accounts, financial accounts accessed online, cryptocurrency and digital currency, domain names, and any other digital property. This authority is granted pursuant to the Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act as adopted in Michigan.

## ARTICLE XI - GOVERNING LAW

This Will shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Michigan.

## ARTICLE XII - SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Will is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

## ARTICLE XIII - EXECUTION INSTRUCTIONS

EXECUTION INSTRUCTIONS: To make this Will legally valid under Michigan law (MCL 700.2502), you must sign this document in the presence of two (2) adult witnesses who are present at the same time. Your witnesses must then sign the Will in your presence. Your witnesses should not be named beneficiaries in this Will. Signing before a notary is required for the self-proving affidavit but does not replace the witness requirement. All three parties (you and both witnesses) should sign at the same time, in the same location.
