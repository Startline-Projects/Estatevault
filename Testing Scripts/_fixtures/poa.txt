# DURABLE POWER OF ATTORNEY

## OF AHMED R. HASSAN

I, AHMED R. HASSAN, a resident of Dearborn, Michigan, being of sound mind, do hereby make, constitute, and appoint the Agent named below as my attorney-in-fact, to act for me and in my name, place, and stead, in the manner set forth in this document.

## SECTION 1 - DURABLE NATURE OF THIS POWER

This power of attorney is durable. It shall not be affected by my subsequent disability or incapacity, and it shall remain in full force and effect until revoked by me in writing.

## SECTION 2 - APPOINTMENT OF AGENT

I appoint RAGA HASSAN, my wife, as my Agent. If RAGA HASSAN is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint KARIM HASSAN, my brother, as my successor Agent.

## SECTION 3 - EFFECTIVE DATE

This power of attorney is effective immediately upon my signing it and delivery to my Agent.

## SECTION 4 - BANKING AND FINANCIAL POWERS

My Agent is authorized to access and manage my bank accounts, make deposits and withdrawals, write checks and authorize electronic transfers, open and close accounts, access safe deposit boxes, and manage certificates of deposit and money market accounts held in my name, including account #4417-002 at Comerica Bank.

## SECTION 5 - REAL ESTATE POWERS

My Agent is authorized to buy, sell, lease, mortgage, manage, and otherwise deal with real property in which I hold any interest, and to execute any deed, mortgage, lease, or other instrument necessary to carry out those transactions.

## SECTION 6 - TAX POWERS

My Agent is authorized to prepare, sign, and file federal, state, and local tax returns on my behalf, to represent me before any taxing authority, and to make any tax election available to me.

## SECTION 7 - FIDUCIARY DUTIES OF AGENT

My Agent accepts the appointment and acknowledges the duty to act in my best interest, to act in good faith, to keep accurate records of all transactions undertaken on my behalf, to keep my property separate from the Agent's own property, and to avoid any transaction involving self-dealing or a conflict of interest.

## SECTION 8 - REVOCATION

I may revoke this power of attorney at any time by written notice delivered to my Agent and to any third party relying on it.

## SECTION 9 - THIRD-PARTY RELIANCE

Any third party may rely upon a copy of this power of attorney as though it were the original. No third party who relies in good faith on the authority of my Agent shall incur any liability to me, my estate, or my successors.

## SECTION 10 - GOVERNING LAW

This power of attorney shall be governed by the laws of the State of Michigan, including MCL 700.5501 and the Michigan Estates and Protected Individuals Code.
